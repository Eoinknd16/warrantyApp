Warranty app — how it works

Tap a topic to open it. Skip straight to whatever you need.

Doing a job

  1. Open the app, tap your name.
  2. Type in the 5-digit WIP number.
  3. Type in the reg. If you've already done this WIP before, it skips straight past this — the reg auto-fills and you go straight to photos. It's still shown on screen so you can double-check it's right.

Got the wrong reg showing? Tap the back arrow, fix it, tap Continue.

  • Take photo / Record video — opens your camera as normal.
  • Add from photos/files — for screenshots or anything already on the phone. You can pick several at once.
  • After each one, you'll be asked what it's of (VIN, Mileage, Fault, LOT, or type your own). This decides the filename.
  • VIN and Mileage only keep one each — take another and it swaps out the old one. Fault, LOT, and Other can have as many as you need.
  1. Tap Send job.
  2. Your phone's share menu opens — pick Mail.
  3. Check the address is filled in, then you must tap Send in Mail yourself — the app can't do this last bit for you.

If you don't actually hit Send in the Mail app, nothing gets filed — the photos aren't lost, but they won't go anywhere until you do.

Notices you might see

🔔 Note — the office left you a message about this job. Tap it to read.

📋 Sent (3) — shows what's already been sent for this WIP on a previous visit, so you know what's still needed.

🔴 PWA required — this job needs Prior Work Approval. When you tap Send, you'll be asked to confirm you've got everything needed before it lets you proceed.

Checking PWA yourself

Tap the 📋 icon in the header, from any screen — it doesn't lose your progress on the job, "Back to job" just returns you exactly where you were.

  1. Pick the job type.
  2. If needed, enter the part value (parts only, not labour).
  3. Estimate the labour time — hours plus 0/15/30/45 minutes.
  4. You'll get one of three answers: PWA needed, check with the office first (it's close to the limit), or no PWA needed — plus what Kia wants photographed for that job type.

If a job's already been flagged by the office, you won't need to run this — the red badge tells you straight away.

Office / admin side

Type the WIP number, type a message, tap Send note. The mechanic sees it the moment they enter that WIP.

  1. Type the WIP number.
  2. Tap "Set required items" to open the checklist.
  3. Tick whichever items this specific job needs.
  4. Tap Save PWA requirement.

The mechanic now sees a red badge on that job, and can't send it without first confirming they've got everything you ticked. Clear flag removes it.

  • Claim value threshold — over this, PWA is always needed.
  • Hourly labour rate — used by the checker's calculator. Rarely needs changing.
  • "Check with the office" buffer — how close to the threshold counts as a borderline case.
  • Job types — add/edit/remove the categories mechanics pick from, and what photos each one needs.
  • PWA requirement items — the master checklist you pick from when flagging a specific job (CVN, LOT, fault video, etc).

Open the "Open jobs" list — search by WIP or reg, or just scroll. Tick the ones you're done with (or use "Select all"), tap Close selected.

This deletes that job's data from the app — reg link, history, notes, PWA flag. It does not touch anything already sitting in SharePoint — that stays exactly as is. This is just about clearing data the app doesn't need to keep anymore.
