Users & permissions

Once you add at least one user, this panel will ask for a PIN before showing anything. Until then, it stays open to everyone \u2014 same as it's always been. Worth reading this plainly: since there's no real backend yet, this is a light gate, not a secure login \u2014 it stops casual access and gives every action a name to attach to, but it isn't a substitute for real authentication.
